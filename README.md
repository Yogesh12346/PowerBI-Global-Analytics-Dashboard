# PROJECT REPORT
# Global Demographics, Economic & Social Development Analysis
## Introduction
### 1.1 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of global demographic trends, economic performance, and social development indicators using interactive data visualization. The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders understand how population, GDP, literacy, and infant mortality vary across countries and regions over time.

### 1.2 Objectives

Analyze global population distribution and growth

Study GDP trends and economic performance across regions

Examine the relationship between economic prosperity and social indicators

Identify disparities in health and education outcomes

Provide data-driven insights through interactive dashboards

## Dataset Description
### 2.1 Data Scope

The dataset includes country-level information covering:

Population

GDP and GDP growth

GDP per capita

Literacy rate

Infant mortality rate

Regional classification

### 2.2 Time Coverage

* Historical data from 1960 onwards

* Interactive year selection (example shown: 2016)

### 2.3 Geographic Coverage

Global (Asia, Africa, Europe, Americas, Oceania)

## Tools & Technologies Used

* Power BI – Dashboard creation and interactivity

* Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation

* DAX – Measures and calculated fields

Microsoft Bing Maps – Geographic visualization

## Dashboard 1: Global Demographics & Economic Overview
### 4.1 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The top section highlights important global metrics:

* Total Population: 27.12 Billion

* Population Growth Rate: 0.01%

* Previous Year GDP: 617.78 Trillion

* Total GDP: 1 × 10¹⁶

* GDP Growth Rate: 1.10%

These KPIs provide a quick snapshot of global demographic and economic conditions for the selected year.

### 4.2 Global Population Distribution (Map)

* Bubble map shows population concentration by country

* Larger bubbles indicate higher population

* Asia shows the highest population density, especially South and East Asia

> Insight: Population is heavily concentrated in Asia, while regions like Oceania show sparse population distribution.

### 4.3 Total Population by Region (Bar Chart)

* East Asia leads in total population

* South Asia and Sub-Saharan Africa follow

* Latin America and other regions show comparatively lower population

> Insight: Developing regions account for the majority of the world’s population.

### 4.4 GDP Trend Over Time (Line Chart)

* GDP has grown steadily since 1960

* Rapid acceleration observed after the 1990s

* Developed regions show consistently higher GDP growth

> Insight: Global economic growth has increased significantly in the last few decades, driven largely by industrialization and globalization.

## Dashboard 2: Health, Education & Economic Quality
### 5.1 Literacy Rate by Region

* Europe & Central Asia show the highest literacy rates

* North America and East Asia also perform well

* Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia lag behind

> Insight: Higher literacy rates strongly correlate with developed regions.

### 5.2 Infant Mortality vs GDP per Capita (Scatter Plot)

X-axis: GDP per Capita

Y-axis: Infant Mortality Rate

Each dot represents a country

> Key Observation:

* Countries with higher GDP per capita tend to have lower infant mortality

* Low-income countries show significantly higher mortality rates

> Insight: Economic prosperity has a strong impact on healthcare outcomes.

### 5.3 Country-Level Detail Table

* The table provides:

* Country

* Region

* Total GDP

* GDP per capita

* Average literacy rate

* Average infant mortality

> Insight:
>Countries like Austria and Andorra show high literacy and low infant mortality, while countries such as Afghanistan and Angola face challenges in healthcare and education.

### 5.4 Infant Mortality Summary Statistics

* Average: 18K

* Minimum: 13.24

* Maximum: 47.15K

* Standard Deviation: 12,508.72

> Insight: Large variation indicates inequality in healthcare access across countries.

## Key Insights & Findings

* Population growth is highest in developing regions

* GDP growth has accelerated globally but remains uneven

* Literacy rate strongly correlates with economic development

* Infant mortality decreases significantly as GDP per capita increases

* Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia face major development challenges

## Business & Policy Implications

* Governments can use such dashboards to prioritize healthcare and education funding

* International organizations can identify high-risk regions

* Economists can track development inequality

* NGOs can focus interventions based on data-driven insights

## Conclusion

* This project successfully demonstrates how data visualization and analytics can transform raw global data into meaningful insights. By integrating demographic, economic, health, and education indicators into a single interactive dashboard, stakeholders can make informed decisions to promote sustainable and inclusive development.
